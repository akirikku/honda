# やらまいか — 本田宗一郎、はじまりの秋

*Yaramaika: Soichiro Honda's first autumn*. A four-page Japanese picture book with narration, character voices
and real recorded sound effects. Read it at **https://akirikku.github.io/honda/**.

Historical fiction: the setting and events follow Honda's own history (Hamamatsu, 1946–47) and Japanese sources; most
dialogue and the two young workers are imagined.

Made on a home Mac with local AI:
- Story: written with Claude from Honda's heritage pages and Japanese sources
- Pictures: Google Gemini (Nano Banana 2)
- Voices: Fish Audio S2 Pro, cloned from samples made with Qwen3-TTS VoiceDesign
- Sound effects: 効果音ラボ (https://soundeffect-lab.info/)
- Furigana: UniDic
